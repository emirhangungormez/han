First Look at Barzakh

Barzakh: Star Gardener is an adventure-puzzle game inspired by The Little Prince, where you encounter a reflection of yourself in different universes.

On your inner journey, you collect stars and grow a garden in your mind. Explore the metaphysical boundaries and discover the poetic spirit of a traveler stranded between worlds.

01. Which game engine is Barzakh developed with?

Barzakh is developed with Unreal Engine 5. This engine offers cinematic quality visuals and the opportunity to create an immersive world.

02. What is Barzakh: Star Gardener?

Barzakh: Star Gardener is an adventure-puzzle game inspired by Sufi mysticism and the poetic spirit of The Little Prince. Emir, stranded in a desert after a plane crash, embarks on a metaphysical journey.

03. What does Barzakh mean?

Barzakh, in Islamic mysticism, refers to the invisible veil between worldly life and the afterlife. In the game, it symbolizes Emir's spiritual journey and his search across universes.

04. When will it be released?

The game is still in development. There is no set release date yet, but you can follow our social media accounts for updates!
